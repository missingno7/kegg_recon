## Deliberately separate same-size records

`IntRect` and `AudioRequest` are both 16 bytes but describe unrelated data. `GameBall` and `FallingSpell`, `ScreenshotSignature` and `TimedLevelChange`, and the 8-byte glyph/frame/value records also remain distinct concepts. Equal size alone is not evidence for a shared type.
